[B]2.1.3[/B]
• Actually fixed the thick bar. An empty texture element does not override Defaults.xml, so every progress and slider kept drawing the default 9-sliced bar - on a 2px line its 6px corners became a slab. Those textures are now set explicitly
• With the slab gone, the rewind colours are visible: accent up to where you are, bright white from there to live

[B]2.1.2[/B]
• Fixed the thick white slab over the timeline and its time labels: the seek slider's background was tinted to fully transparent, which Kodi drew as opaque white. It is left empty now, so only the 2px line shows

[B]2.1.1[/B]
• Timeline is thinner (2px) and now shows how far you have rewound: bright white between your position and live, accent for what you have watched, dim for the rest of the seekable window
• Timeline segments are measured against the seekable window, which is what the bar spans - previously the played part used the programme's scale
• "Watch channel" instead of "Watch live": that button tunes the channel regardless of which programme is selected, so the old label made no sense on a past programme

[B]2.1.0[/B]
• Timeline is now a thin white line with the played part in the accent colour, and the seek position is a small dot that only appears while seeking
• Removed the rounded 9-sliced bars that were rendering as one solid block

[B]2.0.9[/B]
• Rebuilt the timeline: a hairline across the full width, rounded bars over it for buffered and played, and a handle that appears on the seek target while seeking
• Same timeline in the infobar and in the player controls
• Programme information: "Watch live" and "Play from beginning" instead of "Switch" and "Play programme", which read as the same thing

[B]2.0.8[/B]
• Thinner timeline (4px) and a much smaller seek handle
• The timeline's end labels now show the seekable window, which is what the bar actually spans, instead of the programme times
• Says LIVE at the live edge, or how far behind live you are, instead of a bare minus figure
• Arrow seeking is visible again: the bar follows Kodi's own seek target and shows the offset while you tap left/right

[B]2.0.7[/B]
• The timeline can now be used: press up from the player buttons to focus it, then left/right to seek. Live TV scrubs the timeshift/catchup window, recordings and replay seek normally
• The seek handle only stands out once the bar has focus

[B]2.0.6[/B]
• Add-on details: Settings, Dependencies, Update and Versions buttons are back - Kodi provides these controls but leaves their labels to the skin

[B]2.0.5[/B]
• Player buttons are half the size

• Programme information from the guide shows Watch / Play / Record / Add timer / Find similar again - Kodi hides the ones that do not apply but leaves labelling to the skin

[B]2.0.4[/B]
